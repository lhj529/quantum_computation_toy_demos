# Rydberg Quantum Market Maker — Optimal Pulse Control with E[PnL] Objective

A proof-of-concept demonstrating **neutral-atom (Rydberg) quantum computing** applied to **market maker quote-spacing optimization**. The core innovation is optimizing laser pulse waveforms **directly on expected financial return (E[PnL])** rather than quantum fidelity — enabling the "quantum-useful" regime where the optimal solution is not known in advance.

## Problem

A market maker selects which price levels to quote on a non-uniform order book, subject to a **minimum spacing constraint** ($\delta_{\min}=0.30$). Each level has an expected PnL weight $v_i$. This is the **Weighted Maximum Independent Set (MWIS)** problem.

## Approach

1. **Encode** 11 price levels as a 1D chain of $^{87}$Rb Rydberg atoms
2. **Map** non-uniform price spacing to physical distances — the vdW interaction $V_{ij} = C_6 / r_{ij}^6$ naturally enforces the spacing constraint
3. **Encode** PnL weights via additive local detuning: $\Delta_i(t) = \Delta_0(t) + \alpha \cdot v_i \cdot s(t)$
4. **Optimize** 6 waveform parameters via Nelder-Mead to maximize **E[PnL]** estimated by Monte Carlo sampling

## Results

| Metric | PWL Baseline | Optimized | Improvement |
|--------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| E[PnL] | 0.3492 | **0.4964** | +44.1% |
| E[PnL] / Optimal | 64.7% | **91.9%** | +27.2 pp |
| P(optimal bitstring) | 0.15% | **45.20%** | ×300 |
| Unique bitstrings (of 2000 shots) | 235 | **36** | 6.5× more focused |

The optimized pulse parameters: $f_\omega=0.972$, $f_\delta=1.300$, $f_\alpha=3.000$, $T_{\mathrm{neg}}/T=0.320$, $T_{\mathrm{cross}}/T=0.300$, $T_{\mathrm{hold}}/T=0.268$.

## Usage

```bash
python optimal_control_pulse_epnl.py
```

**Shot budget** (configurable at the top of the script):
- Coarse scan: 36 points × 200 shots
- Nelder-Mead: ~183 evaluations × 500 shots
- Final verification: 2000 shots
- Total: ~98k simulations, ~40 seconds

## Key Design Choices

- **E[PnL] objective** over fidelity: No need to know the optimal solution beforehand — directly optimizes the financial metric
- **Additive detuning** over multiplicative: Ensures uniform initial state preparation across all atoms
- **Nelder-Mead** over gradient methods: Noisy Monte Carlo objective — derivative-free methods are more robust
- **Evaluation cache**: Avoids re-sampling identical parameter vectors during simplex contraction

## Why This Matters

Six pulse parameters control an N-atom quantum evolution regardless of N. The optimization cost does **not** scale exponentially with problem size. At N=50–100 (classically intractable, $2^{50} \approx 10^{15}$), this approach provides a natural biased sampler over high-quality portfolios — a genuine path toward quantum advantage in finance.
